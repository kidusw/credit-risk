# Model validation report: `crisk` PD models

*Format: independent model validation, as banks document it under model-risk guidance
(e.g. SR 11-7). Written by the developer for a portfolio project, so it is a
self-assessment, not an independent review. Every number traces to a file in
[`reports/`](../reports) and to the [README](../README.md).*

| | |
|---|---|
| Models | `lending_club` (LightGBM + Platt, real data); `mobile_money` (LightGBM + Platt, **synthetic** data) |
| Use | Application PD → approve/decline at a cutoff, with adverse-action reasons |
| Tier | Would be high (customer-facing credit decisions). Here: demonstration only |
| Outcome | **Approved for demonstration use, with conditions** (findings F1–F6 below) |

## 1. Conceptual soundness

- **Target and population.** Default = charged off or "default". Only matured loans are
  used, so recent vintages can't look safe just because their defaults haven't happened
  yet. 36-month loans only in validation and test, because no 60-month loan in those
  windows had matured.
- **Leakage.** Each of the 151 source columns is classified as an application-time input,
  an outcome or excluded, with a reason (`crisk data audit`). Post-origination fields
  (payments, recoveries, last FICO) are never inputs. The LGD estimate uses recoveries,
  but only as a target-side quantity.
- **Time split.** Train 2007–2014Q2, validation 2014Q3–2015Q1, test 2015Q2–2016Q1. Every
  choice (features, hyperparameters, champion, calibration method, cutoffs, the
  `addr_state` decision) was made on validation. Test was scored once per model.
- **Model form.**
  - Monotonic constraints on 31 features where the direction of risk is settled credit
    practice, so the model can't learn, say, that a higher FICO raises risk. Tests check
    this on predictions.
  - A WoE logistic scorecard is the interpretable benchmark, and LC's own sub-grade is
    the naive one.
- **Geography.** `addr_state` was removed under a rule fixed before the ablation: keep it
  only for ≥ +0.005 validation AUC or ≥ +0.10 pp return. It added +0.0007 and +0.01 pp.
  Zip code is never an input.
- **Thin-file track.** The labels are generated by a documented rule, so conceptual
  soundness here means the pipeline recovers the rule's drivers. It does, for 6 of 8
  drivers within 5 points of share.

## 2. Data quality

- 2,260,668 raw rows, streamed in chunks. Pandera schema checks on the modelling table.
- Sentinels (DTI −1/999) are set to missing. Fields that didn't exist before 2012 stay
  missing for early vintages; LightGBM handles missing values natively.
- **Finding F1 (low):** about 16% of train has no `acc_open_past_24mths` (the field starts
  in 2012Q2). It is harmless to the model but inflates naive PSI. Monitoring uses a
  second reference window.

## 3. Performance

| Test (2015Q2–2016Q1) | AUC | Gini | KS | Brier |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| LC sub-grade (naive) | 0.684 | 0.368 | 0.269 | 0.1227 |
| WoE scorecard + pricing | 0.693 | 0.386 | 0.280 | 0.1221 |
| **LightGBM + pricing (champion)** | **0.706** | **0.412** | **0.301** | 0.1210 |

- **Discrimination:**
  - The lift over the scorecard is +0.013, with a 95% paired-bootstrap CI of
    [0.012, 0.014].
  - It holds on every held-out quarter and within every grade (AUC 0.59–0.65 within A–G).
- **Calibration:** Platt, chosen by cross-validation within validation.
  - On test the PDs under-predict by 1.5 pp: mean 13.7% against a 15.2% default rate.
  - ECE is 0.016 and the slope 1.04.
- **Expected loss:** EL ÷ realised loss is 1.01 on validation and 0.86 on test, falling to
  0.77 in 2016Q1.
  - **Finding F2 (high):** the model under-reserves as credit quality deteriorates.
    Recalibrate on recent vintages before use, and monitor realised vs expected loss
    monthly.
- **Decision value:** at the return cutoff (PD ≤ 0.161), 66% of test loans are approved
  at a 7.11% return on the amount lent, against 6.67% for LC's grades A–B at a similar
  approval rate. On total profit the gain is small (+$2.9M, +0.12 pp), because LC's
  pricing already covered most of the risk.
- **Thin-file (synthetic):** test AUC 0.723, against 0.618 for the rule of thumb and 0.815
  for the oracle. The champion ties the scorecard.

## 4. Stability

- **Score PSI** against train: 0.085 at most on validation, and 0.155 at most on test
  (2015Q4), which is the *monitor* band.
- **Driver:** LC repriced. `int_rate` PSI reaches 0.70 against the last year of train
  (*act*), while `sub_grade` reaches 0.20.
- **Characteristic stability:** the scorecard puts the 2016Q1 applicant 7.1 points
  *safer* than train, yet 2016Q1 defaulted more (16.1% vs 15.5%).
- **Finding F3 (high):** input stability and outcome stability disagree. Lending Club's
  grade meant less over time. A production model needs outcome-based triggers, not only
  PSI.
- **Finding F4 (low):** `term_months` PSI 1.7 is a design artefact (36-month-only
  holdout windows), not drift. It is documented so it isn't actioned.

## 5. Explainability and adverse action

- Exact TreeSHAP for every held-out loan. Each declined applicant gets up to four reasons
  that pushed the PD up, from 19 plain-language codes. Every decline in validation and
  test has at least three.
- **Finding F5 (medium):** for 60% of declines the first reason is Lending Club's own grade
  and rate, which isn't actionable for the applicant. It is flagged
  `actionable=false`, and the applicant still gets up to three borrower-level reasons.
  A lender building its own model would drop the third-party grade, or use the
  borrower-only model (test AUC 0.694).

## 6. Fairness

- **Scope:** no protected attributes are available. The audit covers state, home
  ownership, employment length, income band and zip3 as a proxy check.
- **State:** the minimum AIR is 0.81 once `addr_state` is out of the model, against 0.55
  with it.
- **Income:** under-$40k applicants have AIR 0.40, against 0.55 under LC's grades. Their
  PDs are calibrated within the group (−0.1 pp), so the gap reflects measured risk.
  **Finding F6 (medium):** it is still a disparate-impact exposure that needs a
  documented business justification, or a less discriminatory alternative tested
  (e.g. pricing instead of declining).
- **Thin-file (synthetic demographics):** gender AIR 0.97; rural 0.51; farmers 0.15. The
  gaps are risk-driven (the groups' PDs are slightly low, not high). A seasonal-repayment
  product is the suggested alternative.

## 7. Reject inference

- Lending Club approved about 12% of applications. 71–98% of rejects fall outside the
  accepted range (policy rejects) and stay declined.
- On the three shared columns, in-range rejects score like accepts (score PSI 0.04).
- Fuzzy augmentation changes 2% of decisions; parcelling at 2× changes 8–12%.
- **This cannot be validated without reject outcomes.** It is a sensitivity analysis only.
  The recommended remedy is a small randomised approval test below the cutoff.

## 8. Implementation

- The API serves native LightGBM text files plus JSON metadata, so nothing is
  unpickled. Served PDs equal the training pipeline's to 1e-9 on held-out rows (tested).
- Input validation: unknown features are rejected (422). Missing features are allowed
  and listed in the response.
- Audit trail: every decision is written to Postgres (`decisions`: PD, score, band,
  reasons, inputs, model version). If the database is down, scoring continues and
  `decision_id` is null.
- **Not production-ready:** no authentication, no rate limiting, no model registry or
  approval workflow.

## 9. Monitoring plan

| Metric | Frequency | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Score PSI vs development sample | monthly | > 0.10 review, > 0.25 recalibrate/redevelop |
| Key input PSI (rate, grade, DTI, income, inquiries) | monthly | > 0.25 investigate cause |
| Approval rate vs validation | monthly | ± 5 pp |
| Realised vs expected loss by vintage | quarterly, as vintages season | EL ÷ realised < 0.9 |
| AIR by audited attribute | quarterly | < 0.8, new group below 0.8 |
| Share of declines whose first reason is non-actionable | quarterly | > 50% |

## 10. Conclusion

The models are conceptually sound for their stated, demonstration-only purpose.
Discrimination is modest but real and stable. Calibration drifts with the book (F2, F3),
which is the main condition on any further use. Reject inference and the thin-file track
are presented with their limits stated.
